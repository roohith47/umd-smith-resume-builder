# Past-Tense Action Verb Bank

Pick a verb from here (or an equally concrete past-tense verb not listed) to open any resume
bullet. Prefer the most specific verb that's actually true to what the candidate did — "engineered"
and "architected" are not interchangeable with "helped," even though both are valid choices in
different situations.
